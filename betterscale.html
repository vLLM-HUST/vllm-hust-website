01 / GDN core + metadata builder

Keep mixed work in graphs让 mixed 请求留在图里

Before · 原来 · The native GDN prefill branch gathers and transposes recurrent state, then writes it back; mixed batches also split and rejoin work.原生 GDN prefill 分支取出、转置 recurrent state，计算后再写回；混合批次还需拆分与拼合。

02 / GDN preprocessing + chunk kernels

Do less surrounding work减少核心计算周边的工作

Before · 原来 · Packing Q/K/V, normalization, gates and intermediate layout preparation add small kernels and memory traffic around the core.Q/K/V 整理、归一化、gate 与中间布局准备，在核心计算周围产生小算子和访存。
