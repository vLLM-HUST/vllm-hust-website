Engine & proving ground

Run the engine. Evolve mechanisms through clear interfaces.

vLLM-HUST is an inference engine for domestic compute. Its upstream-compatible runtime also brings hardware backends, compiler stacks, and a fixed benchmark contract together for systems experimentation.
